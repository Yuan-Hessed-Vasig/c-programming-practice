Assignment Operators

Value of an expression computed
	- store in variable for later use
	- C's = (simple assignment) operator is used for that purpose

Simple Assignment
	- The effect of the assignment v = e 
		- to evaluate the expression e and copy its value into value
	- Example
		- i = 5 
		- j = i
		- k = 10 * i + j

Lvalues Example
	-	x
	- arr[5]
	- *ptr
	- emp.salary

Rvalues Example	
	- 42
	- (a + b)
	- &x
